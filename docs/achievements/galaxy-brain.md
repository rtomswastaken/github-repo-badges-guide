# Galaxy Brain Achievement Guide

The **Galaxy Brain** achievement recognizes community members whose answers to questions in **GitHub Discussions** are selected as the **accepted answer**.

## How It Works

GitHub Discussions includes a category format named **Q&A**. In Q&A discussions:
1. A question is posted by a user.
2. Other users submit answers in replies.
3. The original question author or a repository maintainer marks one response as the **Accepted Answer** (green checkmark).

When your response is marked as the accepted answer in a public repository with Discussions enabled, your Galaxy Brain counter increments.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide: Testing or Earning in Your Own Repositories

You can configure Discussions on a repository you own to test or unlock this badge collaboratively:

### 1. Enable GitHub Discussions
1. Go to your repository on GitHub.
2. Click **Settings** > **General**.
3. Under the **Features** section, check the box next to **Discussions**.
4. Click **Set up discussions**.

### 2. Verify Q&A Category Exists
1. Click the **Discussions** tab in your repository.
2. Click **Categories** or **New discussion**.
3. Confirm that the **Q&A** category is enabled (this is the only category type that allows marking accepted answers).

### 3. Ask and Answer
1. Have a collaborator or test account post a question in the **Q&A** category.
2. Post your helpful, complete answer to the question using your primary account.
3. Have the discussion creator click **Mark as answer** under your response.

---

## Rules & Limitations

- **Q&A Category Only:** General discussions, ideas, announcements, and polls do NOT support marked answers and will not contribute toward the badge.
- **Self-Answers:** Marking your own answer to your own question within your own repository may be filtered out by GitHub's anti-gaming heuristics. To ensure the counter increments reliably, provide answers to questions posted by other accounts.
- **Community Discussions:** Active public repositories (such as framework repositories, libraries, or community forums) with active Q&A sections are ideal locations to provide genuine help and earn this badge naturally.
