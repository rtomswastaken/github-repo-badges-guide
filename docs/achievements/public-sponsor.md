# Public Sponsor Achievement Guide

The **Public Sponsor** achievement is awarded to GitHub accounts that actively sponsor an open-source contributor or organization through **GitHub Sponsors**.

---

## Achievement Tier

| Tier | Required Action | Badge Preview |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Default** | Maintain 1 active public sponsorship | <img src="../../Media/Badges/GitHub-Sponsor/PNG/GitHubSponsorBadge.png" width="60"> |

*Note: Public Sponsor is a single-tier status achievement.*

---

## How It Works

GitHub Sponsors allows developers to financially support creators, maintainers, and foundations maintaining open-source software.

To unlock the badge:
1. You must sponsor any eligible user or organization enrolled in GitHub Sponsors.
2. The sponsorship must be set to **Public** visibility. (Private sponsorships do not trigger the public badge).
3. One-time sponsorships and recurring monthly sponsorships both qualify.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Find a Maintainer or Project to Sponsor
1. Navigate to the GitHub profile or repository of a maintainer you appreciate.
2. Look for the pink heart **Sponsor** button on their profile or repository header (e.g., [github.com/sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors)).

### 2. Choose a Sponsorship Tier
1. Select a monthly tier or a one-time sponsorship amount (many creators offer options starting at $1 to $5).
2. On the checkout page, locate the **Visibility** setting.
3. Select **Public** (this makes your sponsorship visible on your profile and awards the badge).
4. Complete the checkout with your payment method.

---

## Badge Longevity & Visibility

- The **Public Sponsor** badge displays in the Achievements section of your profile.
- You will also appear on the maintainer's Sponsors list and on GitHub's sponsor directory.
- If you cancel or remove a sponsorship, the badge may be archived or hidden depending on whether you have other active sponsorships.
